A few questions from after the meeting:

Q: Can I write and also work on art?

Yes! You can get involved with the magazine in as many ways as you want, we do not restrict members to only a certain aspect. Feel free to get involved with every aspect (writing, art & design, photography) if you have the time!

Q: Can I write about XX?

We accept all kinds of writing, including but not limited to: music journalism, long form album review, artist deep-dive / feature article, opinion piece, annotated playlist, interview, live show review. Reach out to Ella or Natalie to get a better picture of what you might write about.

Q: What articles go in print versus online?

Due to space limitations on print, we only choose a few articles that we believe best highlight the theme. Other articles, such as concert reviews or on-theme pieces that couldn't fit, are posted on our website, which has 200+ active viewership on every article.
